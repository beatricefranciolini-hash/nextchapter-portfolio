Reflection

What I would do differently

Test the risky parts first

Test the riskiest and most expensive parts first: job search in Italy failed (2 verified offers), and my own tests were 74% of the AI spend.

Write privacy rules as tests first

The "5 of 5" leak was found late. I would write the edge cases first ("exactly 5 people", "all but one found a job") and build until they pass.

Verify what the AI says

The AI was usually right, which made its mistakes easy to miss: a wrong password, a "closed" sign-up still open, a wrong first diagnosis. Asking it to check the live system, and to plan before changing anything, is what caught them.

Real users earlier

Apart from me, only two people tested it, and only last week, on the finished app. I would show a smaller version to two or three people who are really leaving a job much earlier.

What I would keep

I was often afraid the AI had misunderstood what I meant, so after every step I checked the live site myself to see that it did what I had asked and wanted. I would keep that habit. I now know I can take an idea to a working app and website, and in the end the structure and the agent I had in mind came out even better than I imagined.
