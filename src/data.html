The 5-person threshold, in SQL

when metric <> 'group' and (grp is null or grp < 5)
  then jsonb_build_object('hidden', true)     -- group too small: hide
when v is null or v < 5
  then jsonb_build_object('lt5', true)        -- "fewer than 5"
when metric <> 'group' and grp - v < 5
  then jsonb_build_object('atleast5', true)   -- complement small: "at least 5"
else jsonb_build_object('value', v)           -- safe: show the number

A published figure also changes only when the true value moves by 5 or more, so nobody can spot one person's change overnight. The notebook re-implements both rules in Python.

Limits: test and demo data, 16 users, 4 complete paths, 30 scores. Orders of magnitude, not forecasts.
